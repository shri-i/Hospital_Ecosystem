How booking works

  1. Flask calls CALL book_appointment(...).
  2. The procedure starts a transaction, checks the patient, doctor and date.
  3. If the doctor already has that slot it raises an error and rolls back.
  4. The UNIQUE(doctor_id, date, time) constraint is a second safety net.
